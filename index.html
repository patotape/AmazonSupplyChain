Inbound Transportation

Ocean Freight (Import)
Amazon buys bulk ocean freight capacity. Containers enter via major U.S. seaports: Los Angeles, Long Beach, Seattle, Newark, Savannah, Houston. Includes customs clearance & bonded warehouse options.

Last-Mile Delivery
